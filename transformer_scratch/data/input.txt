The little robot lived in a small house on a quiet street. Every morning the little robot woke up early and opened the window. The warm sun came into the room. The little robot liked the warm sun. The little robot liked the quiet street. The little robot liked the small house.

One day a gray cat came to the window. The cat looked at the robot. The robot looked at the cat. The cat was very quiet. The robot was very quiet too. They looked at each other for a long time.

The robot did not know what to do. The cat did not know what to do. So the robot gave the cat a little piece of bread. The cat ate the bread. The cat liked the bread. The robot liked the cat.

The next morning the cat came back to the window. The robot gave the cat another piece of bread. The cat ate the bread again. The cat was happy. The robot was happy too. They became good friends.

Every day the cat came to the window. Every day the robot gave the cat some bread. Every day they sat together in the warm sun. The cat purred and the robot smiled.

The robot learned that a small kindness can make a big difference. The cat learned that the quiet robot on the quiet street was a good friend. The little house did not feel so small anymore. The quiet street did not feel so quiet anymore.

The robot still liked the warm sun. The robot still liked the small house. But now the robot liked the gray cat most of all.

Time passed and the days grew longer. The sun came earlier and stayed later. The cat came to the window every morning without fail. The robot waited by the window every morning without fail.

One morning the cat did not come. The robot waited and waited. The robot opened the window and looked out at the quiet street. There was no gray cat. The robot was very sad.

The next morning the cat still did not come. The robot did not know what to do. The robot sat by the window and looked at the empty street. The room felt cold without the warm sun. The house felt empty without the cat.

Then in the afternoon, a small sound came from the window. The robot looked up. There was the gray cat, and beside the cat were three little kittens. The cat had not gone away. The cat had been busy. The robot was very happy.

The robot gave the cat some bread. The cat gave the bread to the kittens. The kittens were small and soft and gray like their mother. The robot watched them eat. The room was warm again. The house was full again.

The robot learned that friends do not always stay, but they always come back. The cat learned that the quiet robot had a very big heart. The little house on the quiet street was the best house in the whole world.
